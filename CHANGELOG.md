## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- First version: the websites of an Umami account (Umami Cloud, or a
  self-hosted Umami 2 or 3 with an API key or a user name and password) as
  Gladys devices, with the visitors right now and the visitors, visits, page
  views, bounce rate and most viewed page of the day.
- Scene triggers: visitor spike, first visitor of the day, no visit for a
  while.
- Scene action "Read the figures of a website", with a ready-made sentence.
- Dashboard widget "Umami today".
- "Test the connection" action, texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
